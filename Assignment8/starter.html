Reading Tips & Tricks

Little things that can make reading more enjoyable.

01

Start Small

Try reading just 10-15 minutes a day. You don't have to finish a book all at once!

02

Follow your Interests

Do you love video games, movies or fantasy shows? Look for books with similar themes.

03

Try Different Formats

Not a fan of physical books? Try e-books or audiobooks! You can listen to stories while on the go.

04

Don't Force It

If a book isn't clicking with you, that's okay! Move on and try something else.

There's No Wrong Way to Read

Reading should be enjoyable, not something you feel pressured to do. Take your time, explore, and find stories that make you excited to turn the next page!
