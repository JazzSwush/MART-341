# Keyboard Website
## This website will guide you to making your own keyboard or finding one.

1. Visual Appeal
- I made 6 different icons. I made a hamburger menu bar for future use on the nav bar, a heart for the recommended brands area in the first home page, a keyboard to indicate the layouts page, a bottom casing of a keyboard to indicate the cases page, top view of a switch to indicate the switches page, and the bottom or inside of a keycaps to indicate the keycaps page. I used simple rectangular, square, and circluar shapes to capture the simple essence of keyboard parts.
- I used the same color palatte for my website. I used white for my menu icon since it is sitting on the nav bar, it would match the same colors as the text on nav bar. Then the rest of the colors were a dark slate blue on the other icons. I wanted it to match with the heading that it is supporting. I did not want to add too much detail so I just used squares, rectables, and circles to indicate each page.
- I used the "ledger" font for the body text on the information. I liked the already default fonts I picked for my headers, but added a font for the body text information.

2. Structure & Organization
- I put my table in the layouts page. I wanted to show the different sizes of each layout size. I did so by adding the keys amount to give an idea how big the keyboard they would be building. Then i added a short discription what each keyboard has and what is doesn't have anymore. Just to make the user visualize more what is missing on the keyboard the smaller they go.
- I aligned the top parts and submit button in the center to control the balance of the form. Then, I added other questions to make it feel like justified form in terms of alignment.

3. Design Goals
- My goal for this form was to create a Q&A type of thing. Where I try my best to help them with their keyboard building process by adding a text box for any questions they have about keyboards. The questions help me see where their skill level is at and help them by the way they respond as well.
- My gallery is to show what other people has made for customizing keyboards and building their own. It also gives the user ideas on what they could do to their keyboard. But I did not think it was as important, so I placed them on the bottom of my website. Same for my form because I was not sure where to put it since putting it in my other pages wouldn't make sense and I wanted my home page just general information as well. I could probably make the text bigger for them for being at the bottom of the page.
- I just used a uniformed pattern for my gallery so far. When I have more pictures it will show more in each row and column. I liked how it looked and it looked organized in that way on the page.
